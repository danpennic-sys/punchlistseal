# PunchListSeal Marketing Notes

## Positioning

**PunchListSeal** — a verifiable punch list that survives arguments.

Walkthrough items get disputed: was it open, closed, or never logged? PunchListSeal turns the list into a QuantumGuard evidence pack — location, description, status, owner, optional media hashes, timestamps — that the client can verify offline with the free verifier.

Fits the Operator Pages suite:
- Work logged → FieldSeal / JobSeal
- Photos → MediaSeal
- Punch items → PunchListSeal
- Extra work → ChangeOrderSeal
- Close-out → CloseSeal
- Full job → LifecycleSeal
